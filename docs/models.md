## Selection process notes

The abliterated model selection (see [JOURNAL.md](../JOURNAL.md), 2026-07-30 entry) is the most fully-documented example of the evaluation approach used here: cross-checking a live leaderboard (UGI Leaderboard) rather than trusting SEO-blog claims, verifying the quantizer's reputation, and explicitly preferring a same-base-model option over a higher-parameter alternative from a different family because it de-risks integration. Turning this into a repeatable checklist is an open roadmap item — see [SETUP.md](../SETUP.md#path-forward).
